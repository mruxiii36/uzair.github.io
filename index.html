LT & HT Cubical Panels +

We manufacture highly reliable Low Tension (LT) and High Tension (HT) cubical panels designed for optimal power distribution and safety. These are essential for both receiving power from transformers and safely stepping it down for facility usage.

  • HT Panels: Built to withstand high voltages, incorporating robust switchgear, vacuum circuit breakers (VCBs), and advanced relay protection to prevent systemic electrical faults.
  • LT Panels: Customized for precise secondary power distribution. Features include busbar arrangements, Air Circuit Breakers (ACBs), and power factor correction integration.
  • Rigid CRCA sheet steel enclosures with multi-stage powder coating for long-lasting durability.
Outdoor Feeder Pillars +

Our outdoor feeder pillars provide a safe, secure, and weather-proof localized distribution point for electrical circuits. They are widely used in street lighting networks, housing estates, and industrial complexes.

  • IP54/IP55 rated for high resistance against dust, rain, and harsh weather conditions in the Kashmir valley.
  • Heavy-duty hinges, robust locking mechanisms, and elevated base framing to prevent water ingress.
  • Internally equipped with customized busbars, HRC fuse links, and miniature circuit breakers (MCBs).
Sub Distribution Boards & MCB Boxes +

We fabricate high-quality Residential Sub Distribution Boards (including Safari Make variants) tailored for housing and commercial building load management.

  • Designed for safe division of electrical power feeds into subsidiary circuits.
  • Ample wiring space for easy termination and maintenance.
  • Aesthetic designs suitable for residential mounting with transparent or opaque MCB protection covers.
